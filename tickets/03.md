# 03 · Order: staff can cancel an unpaid, unfulfilled order

**Type:** Test coverage · **Area:** Orders · **Priority:** High

## Context

Cancelling is how staff deal with an order a customer has changed their mind about before it is
paid or shipped. It is reached from the order page's menu and asks for confirmation. There is no
e2e coverage of cancelling an order. A regression here leaves staff with orders they cannot
close and stock that stays allocated.

## What to cover

1. Open the unpaid, unfulfilled order from the orders test data.
2. Cancel it from the order's menu and confirm.
3. The order is cancelled.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] After confirming, the order's status reads "Cancelled".
- [ ] The order can no longer be fulfilled (the fulfil action is not offered).
- [ ] The status is still "Cancelled" after the page is reloaded.

## Notes

- The orders test data already has an unpaid, unfulfilled order; do not create a new one.
