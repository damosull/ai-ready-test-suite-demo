# 07 · Products: staff can rename a product

**Type:** Test coverage · **Area:** Catalogue / Products · **Priority:** Medium

## Context

Merchandisers rename products all the time (a season name, a spelling fix). The name is edited on
the product page and saved with the page's Save button; the page title follows the name. We have
no e2e coverage of the product page, and a rename that silently does not save is exactly the kind
of failure nobody notices until a customer does.

## What to cover

1. Open **E2E Hoodie** from the orders test data.
2. Change its name to "E2E Zip Hoodie" and save.
3. The product carries the new name.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] After saving, the page title shows "E2E Zip Hoodie".
- [ ] After a reload, the name field and the page title both show "E2E Zip Hoodie".

## Notes

- Use the existing orders test data; the product is already there.
