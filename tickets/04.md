# 04 · Order: cancelling a fulfillment puts the order back to unfulfilled

**Type:** Test coverage · **Area:** Orders / Fulfillment · **Priority:** Medium

## Context

Warehouse staff sometimes fulfil an order by mistake, or a parcel is never collected. They then
cancel the fulfillment from the fulfillment card's menu, choosing the warehouse the stock goes
back to. We cover fulfilling (`order-payment-fulfillment.spec.ts`) but not cancelling one.

## What to cover

1. Open the paid and fulfilled order from the orders test data.
2. Cancel its fulfillment, returning the stock to a warehouse.
3. The order goes back to needing fulfillment.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] After confirming, the order's status reads "Unfulfilled".
- [ ] The order offers the fulfil action again.
- [ ] The order is still "Unfulfilled" after the page is reloaded.

## Notes

- The orders test data already has a paid, fulfilled order.
- The cancel dialog asks for a warehouse; any warehouse it offers is fine.
