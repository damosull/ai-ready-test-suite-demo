# 08 · Discounts: staff can remove a rule from a promotion

**Type:** Test coverage · **Area:** Discounts / Promotions · **Priority:** Medium

## Context

A promotion is made of rules (which products, which channel, how much off). Marketing removes a
rule when an offer ends early for one part of the catalogue but the promotion itself carries on.
Removing a rule is done from the rule's delete button on the promotion page, after a
confirmation, and takes effect straight away. There is no e2e coverage of promotions.

## What to cover

1. Open the **E2E Tee sale** catalogue promotion from the orders test data. It has one rule.
2. Delete that rule and confirm.
3. The promotion remains, with no rules.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] After confirming, the promotion shows no rules.
- [ ] After a reload, the promotion still shows no rules, and it is still the "E2E Tee sale"
      promotion (it was not deleted).

## Notes

- Use the existing orders test data; the promotion is already there.
