# 01 · Draft order: staff can apply a manual percentage discount to the whole order

**Type:** Test coverage · **Area:** Orders / Drafts · **Priority:** Medium

## Context

Staff taking an order by hand sometimes agree a discount with the customer ("10% off as a
goodwill gesture"). They add it from the draft's summary as a manual order discount, either a
percentage or a fixed amount, with a reason. The manual discount sits on top of any catalogue
promotion already on the lines. We cover promotions on drafts (`draft-pricing.spec.ts`) but not
manual discounts, and the arithmetic of a percentage applied after a line promotion is exactly
the kind of thing that regresses quietly.

## What to cover

On a draft order in the USD channel with one **E2E Tee** (which has the "E2E Tee sale"
catalogue promotion, and on its own does not reach the order promotion's threshold):

1. Add a manual order discount of **25%** with a reason.
2. The draft total drops by 25% of the already-promoted subtotal.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The draft total equals the tee's price after its catalogue promotion, less 25%.
- [ ] The discount breakdown shows the manual discount as 25%, alongside the line promotion.
- [ ] The discounted total is still there after the page is reloaded (saved, not just shown).

## Notes

- The tee's price and its promotion are already in the orders test data.
- No new test infrastructure needed.
